Separate the rounds, show members their own result

Recommendations for two points from Stefan's feedback: (3) the monthly competitions are not separated, and (4) it's hard to see your own result, and nobody is told their rank when a round ends.

The mockups use real entries and vote counts from community.swissphotoclub.com, read on 25 October 2026.

What the site does today

The biggest finding is good news. Discourse already renders a page per round, ranked by votes, in the masonry layout. Nothing links to it, and it shows no title, no winner and no rank.

All rounds share one listThe Monthly Challenge category currently holds 14 October, 38 September and 15 August topics in a single feed, newest first. Only the round tag tells them apart, and you can't see where one month ends and the next begins.
A per-round page already exists/tags/c/monthly-challenge/6/2026-09-frame-in-fra/21?order=votes shows only September, sorted by votes, in masonry. The staff "Crown the winner" panel already reads this same listing. Members never reach it.
No rank is shown anywhereA card shows its vote count. To learn your place, you'd have to scroll 37 entries and count. Stefan's September entry has 1 vote, which is a shared 19th of 37. He had no way to see that.
Nothing tells entrants the resultWhen a round closes, Topic Voting tells voters their votes were returned. Entrants get no message about the result, and the winner only appears in the hero.
0 · The August winner is not the entry with the most votes"Und hopp" was crowned with 4 votes, behind "BnW Crow" (6) and "J'ai raison" (5). The voting guide says "most valid votes" wins. A public ranking built from raw counts would show the winner in 3rd place. Before any rank goes live, decide what counts as valid (self-votes? staff votes? a jury tie-break?) so the ranking and the crown use the same rule.
Round names are cut offDiscourse's tag length limit (20 characters) turns "Frame in Frame" into 2026-09-frame-in-fra and October's German tag into 2026-10-gegenüberste. These cut-off names show in the sidebar and on tag pages. The brief topic also carries the round tag, so it appears in the round's list and collects votes (October's brief has 1).
Point 3

Separate the rounds

  • Members with more than one entry get one line per entry, best first.
  • During an open round, show "Your entries: 2 of 3 · 4 votes so far", as in the 3.1 mockup, but no rank. A live rank invites tactical voting and discourages people who are behind early. The entry count reuses challenge_max_entries_per_round, which the submit form already checks.

4.2

A personal results message when the winner is crowned

Stefan asked for an email that "reaches the competitors and shows them their own rank". Send it as a Discourse personal message from SPC Bot. Discourse then emails it according to each member's own notification settings. It also stays in their inbox on the site, and it respects unsubscribes without extra work.

How it would run

  • A theme can't send mail, so this is a small Rails runner script in server/, deployed exactly like challenge-vote-reset.rb: a systemd timer on the host, read-only by default, and it only sends with SPC_APPLY=1.
  • Trigger: a completed round that has a topic with the winner tag. Crowning in the staff panel is then the only action staff take. The timer sends the messages within the hour, and no new button is needed.
  • Sends once: mark the round as sent (a custom field on the brief topic) before sending, so a rerun or an interrupted run never sends twice.
  • One message per entrant, with all of their entries in it, in the language they use the site in (de/en/fr). The rank comes from the same rule as the page in 3.2.
  • Non-entrants: if everyone should hear about the winner too, as your original note suggested, post one topic in Announcements. Members who watch that category get it by email. That's cheaper and less noisy than a message to everyone.

Discourse Automation can send messages, but not one that's personalised with a computed rank. That's why this is a custom script rather than an Automation rule.

Questions for you

  • Why was "Und hopp" crowned in August with 4 votes when two entries had more? Was it a jury pick, invalid votes, or votes changing after the crown?
  • Should vote counts stay visible on cards while a round is open? Hiding them until results would reduce bandwagon voting. That's a separate decision, but it's closely related.
  • Should results messages go only to entrants (recommended), with an Announcements post for everyone else?

What we can learn from fotocommunity

Eight recommendations drawn from Stefan Meury's feedback and a review of fotocommunity.de. Every mockup uses real content from community.swissphotoclub.com: real rounds, entries, vote counts and members, taken from the live site today.

01

A gallery per challenge round

Today every round sits in one Monthly Challenge list, and only the tag tells them apart. On fotocommunity each month is its own gallery with a cover image, grouped by year. We already tag every entry with its round, and the winner carries the winner tag, so this is mostly display work.

StefanThe monthly competitions are not separated.
fotocommunityContests → Monthly → 2026 / 2025 / Archive. One cover-image card per round. Open, in-voting and closed rounds are listed separately.
BuildA "Rounds" strip under the challenge hero. Each card links to the round's tag page. The cover is the winner, or the most-voted entry while the round is open. The data comes from the round list setting (challenges) plus the tag listings the hero already fetches.

02

Round timeline in the hero, and fix the cut-off round name

fotocommunity's contest page gives three explicit dates: upload until, voting from–to, results on. Our round has one phase (submit and vote until the 31st), but a member can't see what happens after that. A three-step timeline answers "where are we, and when will I know?" at a glance.

StefanNot easy to see … competition galleries.
Bug found while preparing thisThe live hero reads "SEPTEMBER WINNER · FRAME IN FRA". The theme name is rebuilt from the tag, and Discourse cut the tag off at 20 characters (2026-09-frame-in-fra). October has the same problem (2026-10-juxtapositio).
BuildUse the round's title from the round list setting when it exists, and the tag only as a fallback (roundThemeLabel() in spc-monthly-challenge.js). Render the timeline from the round's start, deadline and announcement dates.

03

"My entries" panel and a personal results email

fotocommunity's Voting Centre has personal tabs: my photos, my votes, history. Stefan asked for exactly this, plus an email at the end of each round with your own rank. The panel is theme work. The email is not: a theme cannot send mail, so it needs a server-side job (Discourse Automation or a script in spc-be) that runs when staff add the winner tag.

StefanIt's difficult to see your own result, and there is no email at the end of a competition … that shows them their own rank.
fotocommunityVoting Centre → "My photos". The top 3 per contest go into the Popular gallery and the top 10 get a blog post.
BuildThe panel goes on category 6 for signed-in members and reads the round tag listings already cached. Rank is by votes, with ties shared. The email uses the same numbers.

04

Homepage rows per section, one standard card

Today the homepage shows one masonry feed that mixes challenge entries, feedback requests and introductions. Each card carries extra lines for category, tag and votes. fotocommunity separates what (sections) from how chosen (new, popular, feedback requested) and gives every gallery the same card. At our size, the equivalent is a few named rows, each with "See all".

StefanThe page itself is constructed and laid out very complexly … unsorted and also not standardized.
fotocommunityPhotos menu: subject sections on the left, curated views on the right. Every grid card shows only the photo, title and author.
BuildReplace the mixed homepage feed with 3 rows: current challenge, feedback requests, new members. One card component: photo, title, author. Category and tag move off the card, because the row heading already says them.

05

Wider photo grid and a dark photo stage

fotocommunity's photos aren't much larger per tile than ours. They feel bigger because the rows run edge to edge, every photo in a row has the same height and nothing is cropped, the cards carry almost no text, and single photos sit on a dark stage. Keep text at 1110px and let only the photos break out.

StefanNot standardized, so it's not easy to see categories or competition galleries.
fotocommunityJustified rows with a 4–6px gap. Title and author appear on hover or in one small line. The photo page is a full-width dark stage with prev/next arrows.
Build, with careTopic List Thumbnails positions every card with JS, so the canvas width can only change if it is verified on categories 6, 7, 8 and 12. Start with a devtools prototype on category 7, on a laptop and on a large monitor.

06

Show membership status

On fotocommunity, the tier is printed under every member's name ("Premium (Pro), Bern") and premium content carries a gold mark. On our site you can't see whether you are a member, on trial or lapsed, which is what Stefan missed on the settings page. Group 40 already tells the theme member vs non-member. Dates (since, renewal, trial end) live in spc-be and would have to be exposed to Discourse, for example as a user field.

StefanThe settings page is not very useful and doesn't show the user status.
fotocommunityA tier and city line under the name on the profile, photo pages and comments. A clear tier table on the membership page.
BuildA member/trial badge on the user card and profile header, from group 40. A status block at the top of /profile-setup: status, renewal and what's included, with a "Manage membership" link to spc-be.

07

Photo of the Week

fotocommunity has a ladder of recognition beyond its contests: Photo of the Hour, Editors' Choice and the member-voted Gallery. A small club can't fill hourly slots, but a weekly staff pick from Photo Feedback, with one line of why, rewards the critique side of the club, which a monthly winner never does. It reuses the winner-tag pattern: staff add a photo-of-the-week tag, and the homepage shows the newest one.

08

A visitor homepage that leads with what makes us different

fotocommunity's visitor homepage opens with trust badges (25 years, your rights stay yours, data protection, servers in Germany) and one call to action. It sells size. We can't win on size or price, as Stefan says, so we should lead with what they lack: their comments are mostly short praise, while ours are structured critique. Add Swiss meetups, three languages and live webinars.

StefanIt's hard to compete with the price and the smaller range … So it has to have unique features.
fotocommunity512,461 photos flagged "constructive feedback requested", and most of them show 0–1 comments. Feedback is their weak spot.
BuildThe anonymous homepage hero: a member photo as background, one call to action, four trust points underneath. The non-member banner keeps its role on other pages.

What not to copy

Sprawl. 25+ forums, 11 AI sections and six subdomains, each with its own design. The contest brief is a blog post and the entries live in the app. Our single site is an advantage.
Charging by upload volume. Their tiers are priced by photos per week. For a small club, membership should buy the critique exchange and the events, not storage.
Praise-only comments. Their comment threads are mostly "schön, LG". Our give-to-get exchange exists to avoid exactly this, so keep its rules visible.
SEO tag spam and ads. Their footer lists about 90 keyword links. That costs trust and doesn't help a members' club.

Open questions before building

  1. How is a winner decided? Most votes, or staff choice? August's winner had fewer votes than another entry. Ranks in #3 depend on the answer.
  2. Who sends the results email? Discourse Automation on the server, or spc-be, which already emails members.
  3. Which membership dates can Discourse see? Group 40 says member or not. Since, renewal and trial end need a field synced from spc-be.
  4. Book Stefan's walkthrough. He offered a call to show what feels complex. It would confirm #4 and #5 before we spend time on them.
